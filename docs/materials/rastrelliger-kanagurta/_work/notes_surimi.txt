Fish 60-70 g market Thasala, Nakhon Si Thammarat; Khanom coast; offloaded ~12h after capture, iced 1:2, gutted filleted skinned, minced.
Unwashed mince moisture ~75%; pH 6.31; myoglobin unwashed 6.77 mg/g sample; W1 2.56, W2 1.63, W3 1.61 mg/g. metMb ~73-76%. Soret peak 407 nm.
Myoglobin extracted cold 40 mM phosphate buffer pH 6.8 (Chaijan); spectra visible & Soret, Shimadzu UV-2100; proportions by P1=A582/A525, P2=A557/A525, P3=A503/A525.
Lipid Bligh & Dyer. yield 80-90%. gel strength W2/W3 ~440 g.cm (Thai std >=400). expressible drip unwashed 8.14%, W1 5.37%. whiteness unwashed 65.11.
